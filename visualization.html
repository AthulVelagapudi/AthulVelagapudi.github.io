Plots from the Government R&D expenditure analysis (2018-2021)

Top 10 economic activities with highest R&D expenditure across years
From Bar Plot Analysis: Professional, Scientific and Technical Activities dominate R&D spending across all years, followed by Agriculture, Forestry and Fishing. The top sectors account for the majority of total expenditure and show a peak in 2019-20 with a decline in 2020-21.
Scatter plots: correlation analysis (2018-2021)
From Scatter Plot Analysis: Year-pair correlations are extremely high (r > 0.998), indicating that sectors maintain their relative spending positions across years and showing a strong linear relationship between fiscal-year expenditures.
Box plots: R&D Expenditure distribution analysis
From Box Plot Analysis: Distributions are right-skewed with large IQRs. Agriculture and Professional/Scientific activities appear as outliers, reflecting substantial spending disparities across sectors.
Boxplot - Expenditure across all years
Temporal Trends: Expenditure peaks in 2019-20 and falls in 2020-21 (overall -11.72% from 2019-20). Median values remain relatively stable but extreme values drive the skew.
Boxplot - Top 10 activities
Top 10 Focus: The top 10 activities concentrate most R&D funding; Professional/Scientific and Agriculture dominate, showing policy focus and sectoral concentration of investment.
Scatter: 2018-19 vs 2019-20
2018-19 vs 2019-20: Very high correlation (r = 0.999). Most sectors increased or held positions in 2019-20 compared to 2018-19, contributing to the overall 7.11% increase.
Scatter: 2019-20 vs 2020-21
2019-20 vs 2020-21: Extremely strong correlation (r = 0.998) despite an overall decline in total expenditure; sector ranks remain stable even as absolute values fall.
Scatter: 2018-19 vs 2020-21
2018-19 vs 2020-21: Correlation remains very high (r = 0.998), underscoring spending consistency across years and indicating predictable sectoral allocation patterns.
